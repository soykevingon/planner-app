Kairo is an early test (alpha). It's a free proof of concept made by one person and shared with a small group of invited testers. Use it to try it and give feedback, not as the only place for anything important.

Terms of use

  • Invitation only. You need an invite code to create an account. You must be 18 or older.
  • No guarantees. Kairo is provided "as is". It may have bugs, change without notice, be unavailable, lose data, be reset or be shut down. Keep your own copy of anything important (Settings → Backup → Export backup).
  • Free. There is no charge during the alpha. If Kairo ever becomes a paid service, you'll be told first and nothing will be charged without your clear agreement.
  • Use it fairly. Don't use Kairo for anything illegal, to harm others, or to try to break, overload or get into parts of the service or other people's data. Don't store very sensitive information (passwords, card or bank numbers, health records, government IDs).
  • Your content is yours. You keep ownership of what you write and upload. You give Kairo only the permission needed to store it, sync it to your devices and show it to the people you choose to share with.
  • Feedback. Ideas you send may be used to improve Kairo, with no obligation to you.
  • Ending. You can delete your account at any time. Access can be removed for anyone who breaks these terms, and the alpha can end at any time.
  • Liability. As far as the law allows, the maker of Kairo is not liable for losses from using it, including lost data or missed deadlines.
  • Law. These terms follow the laws of Puerto Rico.

Privacy

What is stored

  • Your account: email, a protected (hashed) password, your name and the invite code you used.
  • What you add: tasks, projects, events, spaces, settings and attached files.
  • Feedback you send, and short automatic error reports (app version, where the error happened, the error text and your browser type). Error reports are deleted after 30 days.

Where it lives and who can see it

  • Data is stored with Supabase (a cloud database service), whose servers may be outside Puerto Rico. The app files are served by GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. These services may see your IP address and basic browser details.
  • A copy is kept on your device so Kairo works without internet. Signing out removes it from that device.
  • You see your data. People you share a space with see what's in that space.
  • The administrator (the person who runs this alpha) technically has access to the database. That access is used only to keep the service working, fix problems you report, or when the law requires it. Your data is not read for any other reason.
  • Voice input: when you use the microphone, your browser's own speech service (for example Google or Apple) turns your voice into text. Kairo only receives the text.
  • No ads, no selling or renting of your data, no tracking or analytics tools.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete my account and data removes your login, tasks, events, spaces, files, feedback and error reports right away. Spaces you created and shared are deleted too; the other members keep their own tasks.
  • There are no extra backups kept during the alpha, so deleted data can't be recovered.

Changes and contact

  • If these terms change, the app will tell you and show the new version.
  • Questions or requests: use Send feedback in the app (profile menu), or ask the person who invited you.

Privacidad

Qué se guarda

  • Tu cuenta: email, una contraseña protegida (cifrada), tu nombre y el código de invitación que usaste.
  • Lo que añades: tareas, proyectos, eventos, espacios, ajustes y archivos adjuntos.
  • Los comentarios que envíes, y reportes cortos y automáticos de errores (versión de la app, dónde pasó, el texto del error y tu tipo de navegador). Los reportes de errores se borran a los 30 días.

Dónde se guarda y quién lo ve

  • Los datos se guardan en Supabase (un servicio de base de datos en la nube), cuyos servidores pueden estar fuera de Puerto Rico. Los archivos de la app los sirve GitHub Pages. Las letras (fuentes) vienen de Google Fonts. Estos servicios pueden ver tu dirección IP y datos básicos del navegador.
  • Se guarda una copia en tu dispositivo para que Kairo funcione sin internet. Al cerrar sesión se borra de ese dispositivo.
  • Tú ves tus datos. Las personas con quienes compartes un espacio ven lo que hay en ese espacio.
  • El administrador (la persona que maneja esta alpha) técnicamente tiene acceso a la base de datos. Ese acceso se usa solo para mantener el servicio funcionando, arreglar problemas que reportes o cuando la ley lo exija. Tus datos no se leen por ninguna otra razón.
  • Voz: cuando usas el micrófono, el servicio de voz de tu propio navegador (por ejemplo Google o Apple) convierte tu voz en texto. Kairo solo recibe el texto.
  • Sin anuncios, sin vender ni alquilar tus datos, sin herramientas de rastreo ni analítica.

Borrar tus datos

  • Settings → Delete my account and data borra al momento tu acceso, tareas, eventos, espacios, archivos, comentarios y reportes de errores. Los espacios que creaste y compartiste también se borran; los demás miembros conservan sus propias tareas.
  • Durante la alpha no se guardan copias adicionales, así que lo borrado no se puede recuperar.
