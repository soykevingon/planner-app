Terms and Privacy

Delete your account?

This permanently deletes, from this device and from the cloud:

  • your tasks, projects, events, spaces and settings
  • your attached files
  • spaces you created and shared (the other members keep their own tasks)
  • your feedback and error reports
  • your login

It can’t be undone. Want a copy first? Use Settings → Backup → Export backup.
